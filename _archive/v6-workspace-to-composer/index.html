How to review

  1. Click a timestamp to jump the video to that line.
  2. Use Comment on a line, or select any words in the script and choose Comment on selection.
  3. Pause anywhere and use Comment at this moment; it attaches to the line being spoken.
  4. To rewrite a line, use Suggest edit. The original stays put; your version shows as a redline with your name, and everyone's changes are listed under Changes.
